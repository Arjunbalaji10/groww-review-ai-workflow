# Groww — Weekly Product Pulse

**Period:** 12–18 October 2026  
**Evidence:** Preliminary analysis of 865 classified reviews from 27 July–18 October 2026.

## Key themes

1. **High fees and charges:** 26 classified reviews; 20 rated 1–2 stars. Users raised concerns about brokerage costs and charge transparency.
2. **Bugs and crashes:** 20 classified reviews; 13 rated 1–2 stars. Reports included onboarding and application-flow problems.
3. **Customer support:** 15 classified reviews; 12 rated 1–2 stars. Some users reported unresolved issues and inadequate support.

## Customer voice

- **Fees:** “worest broker charges to High customer support nill” — 1 star.
- **Application flow:** “with correct name as per pan it says incorrect, also just option to do exis or continue application, no option for customer support.” — 1 star.
- **Support:** “0 support from customer support team my issue was not solved” — 1 star.

## Key observation

Within the classified sample, fee concerns and reports of unresolved support issues appear repeatedly. The fee-related candidate review set also contains reports of unclear charges. These are user-reported experiences, not independently verified billing errors.

**Data limitation:** Only 865 of 12,835 collected reviews (6.74%) have been classified. These results are preliminary and may not represent all Groww reviews.

## Recommended Actions

- **Improve fee transparency:** Make the applicable charge breakdown easier to find before and after a trade.
- **Investigate onboarding failures:** Reproduce PAN-name validation failures and ensure users have a clear recovery or support path.
- **Improve issue resolution:** Review support complaints and measure resolution time and repeat contacts.
