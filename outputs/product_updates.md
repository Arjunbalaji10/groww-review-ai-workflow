# Groww Product Update

**Reporting period:** 12–18 October 2026
**Approval status:** Pending human review

## Evidence
This preliminary analysis covers 865 classified reviews out of 12,835 collected reviews (6.74%). Findings are directional and may not represent all users.

## Priority 1: Fee transparency
- **Signal:** 26 classified reviews concerned high fees and charges; 20 received 1–2 stars.
- **Action:** Review where users can find the applicable trading-charge breakdown before and after a trade.
- **Success measures:** Track fee-related complaint rate, charge-page visits, and repeat fee-related contacts.
- **Guardrail:** Do not treat review complaints as proof of incorrect billing.

## Priority 2: Application reliability
- **Signal:** 20 classified reviews concerned bugs and crashes; 13 received 1–2 stars.
- **Action:** Investigate reported onboarding and PAN-name validation failures.
- **Success measures:** Track onboarding failure rate, crash rate, and successful application completion.

## Priority 3: Customer support
- **Signal:** 15 classified reviews concerned customer support; 12 received 1–2 stars.
- **Action:** Review unresolved complaints and ensure users can find a clear support path.
- **Success measures:** Track first-response time, resolution time, and repeat contacts.

## Customer communication
Explain that applicable charges depend on the relevant transaction and account context. Direct users to Groww's official pricing and charge-breakdown resources. Ask users with transaction-specific concerns to contact official support.
